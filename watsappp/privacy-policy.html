Privacy Policy for WhatsApp Direct

Effective Date: October 2023

Introduction

Welcome to WhatsApp Direct. Your privacy is critically important to us. This Privacy Policy outlines how your information is handled when you use our web application.

Our core privacy principle is that **your data is yours**. This application is designed to be a client-side tool, meaning most operations and data storage happen directly in your browser on your own device.

Information We Handle

1. Phone Number History

If you enable the "Save numbers" feature, the country code and phone number you open are stored exclusively in your browser's local storage (`localStorage`) on your device.

  • This data is never transmitted to our servers or any third party.
  • You have full control over this data and can clear your entire history at any time using the "Clear all" button.
  • If you disable the "Save numbers" feature, no history is ever stored.

2. Analytics (Anonymous)

We use Google Analytics (via Firebase) to collect anonymous usage data. This helps us understand how the app is being used so we can improve it. The information collected is aggregated and includes:

  • General usage events, such as opening the app or clicking a button.
  • The country code selected (to understand our user base).
  • Whether the app is used in a browser or as an installed Progressive Web App (PWA).

This data is completely anonymous and cannot be used to identify you personally. We do not track the phone numbers you enter in our analytics.

3. Push Notifications

We use OneSignal to provide optional push notifications. If you choose to opt-in, OneSignal will store a unique token for your device to send you messages. We do not link this token to any personal information or phone numbers you enter. You can disable notifications at any time through your browser settings.

4. Advertisements

This application displays ads from Google AdSense to support its development.

  • Google and its partners may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites on the internet.
  • You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings.

Data Security

We take reasonable measures to ensure the application is secure. Since your sensitive data (phone number history) is stored on your device, the security of your own device and browser is also crucial. We do not have access to this local data.

Your Choices and Control

  • History: You control whether history is saved via the "Save numbers" toggle and can delete it at any time.
  • Notifications: You can choose whether to enable or disable push notifications through your browser.
  • Cookies & Ads: You can manage your ad preferences and cookies through your browser settings and the Google Ads Settings link provided above.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically for any changes.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us.
